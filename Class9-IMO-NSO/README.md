## Worksheet production standard

For every chapter, create:
1. **50 original questions** in four-option MCQ format.
2. A mix of easy, medium, hard, and HOTS/application questions.
3. Answer key at the end (not directly after each question).
4. Short, correct explanations for all answers, with calculations/units where relevant.
5. Source/syllabus mapping and a last-reviewed date.
6. A quick self-score tracker and a note to revisit incorrect answers.

Never copy a whole commercial worksheet or paid question bank. Official sample papers should guide syllabus and exam pattern, not be republished as original content.

## Suggested 10-minute daily routine

- 1 minute: recall key facts/formulae.
- 7 minutes: solve a focused set under a timer.
- 2 minutes: check answers and write down the reason for each mistake.

For a full 50-question chapter set, split it into five 10-question timed sessions, then complete a mixed revision quiz.
