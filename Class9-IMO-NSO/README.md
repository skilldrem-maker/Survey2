**Status (last checked: 10 October 2026): guide only.** This repository entry is a preparation guide, not the completed worksheet collection. Chapter worksheets, answer keys, PDFs, and mixed mock papers have **not** yet been created.

## Worksheet production standard

For every chapter/lesson:
1. Create **50 original questions** in four-option MCQ format.
2. Include a balanced mix of easy, medium, hard, and HOTS/application questions.
3. Put the answer key at the end, not directly after each question.
4. Add concise explanations for every answer, including calculations and units where relevant.
5. Map each question to the relevant syllabus topic and add a last-reviewed date.
6. Add a self-score tracker and space to note mistakes.

Use official sample papers to understand the syllabus and exam pattern; do not reproduce their complete question sets as newly authored content.

## Suggested 10-minute daily routine

- 1 minute: recall key facts and formulae.
- 7 minutes: solve a focused set under a timer.
- 2 minutes: check answers and record why mistakes happened.

For a 50-question chapter set, split practice into five 10-question timed sessions, then complete a mixed revision quiz.
