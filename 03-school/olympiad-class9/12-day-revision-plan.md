# 12-Day Class 9 IMO + NSO/ISO Revision Plan

This is a high-intensity review plan. It does not replace completing every chapter bank; use the chapter tracker to make sure no syllabus topic is skipped.

| Day | Mathematics / IMO | Science / NSO-ISO | Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Number systems, polynomials | Motion, force | Record baseline errors |
| 2 | Algebraic identities, linear equations | Gravitation, work and energy | Reattempt Day 1 errors |
| 3 | Coordinate geometry, sequences | Sound | 20-question mixed quiz |
| 4 | Lines and angles, triangles | Matter around us | Review formula/concept sheet |
| 5 | Quadrilaterals, areas | Pure substances and mixtures | Reattempt wrong questions |
| 6 | Circles, constructions | Atoms and molecules | Timed mini-test |
| 7 | Area/perimeter, surface area/volume | Structure of atom | Error-log review |
| 8 | Statistics, probability | Cell, tissues | Mixed MCQs |
| 9 | Euclid geometry, reasoning, HOTS | Diversity of living organisms | Timed mini-test |
| 10 | Full IMO mixed paper | Human health and diseases | Analyse every wrong answer |
| 11 | IMO weak chapters + Achievers-style problems | Improvement in food resources + weak science chapters | Reattempt all starred items |
| 12 | Full timed revision and formula/rule recall | Full timed revision and concept recall | Sleep adequately; no all-night cramming |

## Daily method
1. 10 minutes: recall formulas, definitions, diagrams, or laws from memory.
2. 25–40 minutes: solve a chapter worksheet without notes.
3. 15 minutes: check the key and write why each error happened.
4. 10 minutes: redo wrong questions without seeing the solution.
5. Keep a “mistake log” with columns: chapter, question ID, error type, correct idea, reattempt date.

## Error types
- Concept gap
- Misread question
- Formula/units
- Calculation
- Option elimination
- Time pressure
- Guessing

## Exam strategy
- First solve questions you can confidently do.
- Do not spend too long on one question during the first pass.
- Underline qualifiers such as *not*, *incorrect*, *always*, *only*, and units.
- For science numericals, write the formula and SI units before substituting values.
- For geometry, mark given information on a diagram and state the theorem used.
- For IMO/NSO Achievers questions, slow down and check each inference rather than guessing quickly.
