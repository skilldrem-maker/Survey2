Class 9 • Original practice • 10 questions per chapter

## 15. Surface Areas and Volumes
1. Volume of a cube of side 4 cm: A 16 B 32 C 64 D 256 cm³
2. Total surface area of a cube of side a: A a² B 4a² C 6a² D a³
3. Volume of a cuboid 5×4×3 cm: A 12 B 20 C 60 D 120 cm³
4. Curved surface area of a cylinder is: A πr²h B 2πrh C 2πr² D πr²
5. Volume of a cylinder with r=2 cm and h=7 cm (π=22/7): A 44 B 88 C 154 D 176 cm³
6. If each dimension of a cube doubles, its volume becomes: A 2 times B 4 times C 6 times D 8 times
7. A cuboid has volume 120 cm³, length 6 cm and width 5 cm. Height: A 2 B 3 C 4 D 5 cm
8. Lateral surface area of a cube of side 3 cm: A 9 B 18 C 27 D 36 cm²
9. A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. Volume is: A 30π B 60π C 90π D 180π cm³
10. The volume of a sphere of radius r is: A 4πr² B 4/3πr³ C πr²h D 2πr

## 18. Reasoning and Mathematical HOTS
1. Find next: 2,6,12,20,30,… A 36 B 40 C 42 D 44
2. If CAT is coded DBU by shifting each letter one forward, DOG becomes: A EPH B CNG C EOG D FPH
3. Which does not belong? A square B triangle C circle D cube
4. Find the missing number: 3, 6, 12, 24, __ A 30 B 36 C 48 D 60
5. A is taller than B; B taller than C. Who is shortest? A A B B C C D cannot tell
6. If 4 workers complete a job in 6 days at equal rates, 8 workers need: A 2 days B 3 days C 6 days D 12 days
7. All roses are flowers. Some flowers fade quickly. Which must be true? A all roses fade quickly B some roses fade quickly C all roses are flowers D no flowers are roses
8. If today is Tuesday, what day is 10 days later? A Thursday B Friday C Saturday D Sunday
9. A number is multiplied by 3 and then 5 is added to give 26. Number: A 5 B 6 C 7 D 8
10. In a class, every student studies Math or Science or both. 18 study Math, 15 Science, 7 both. Total students: A 26 B 33 C 40 D 40?
