# NSO/ISO Practice Bank — Part 1: Physics
**Class 9 | Original practice questions | 10 questions per chapter**

Try without notes. Use SI units. Answer key is at the end.

## Chapter 1 — Motion

1. A car travels 150 m in 10 s at uniform speed. Its speed is: A) 10 m/s B) 15 m/s C) 20 m/s D) 25 m/s
2. Which quantity can be zero even when distance travelled is non-zero? A) speed B) distance C) displacement D) time interval
3. A body starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 s. Final speed: A) 5 m/s B) 7 m/s C) 10 m/s D) 12 m/s
4. The slope of a displacement–time graph represents: A) acceleration B) speed/velocity C) force D) momentum
5. A runner completes one full 400 m lap and returns to the start. Displacement is: A) 0 m B) 200 m C) 400 m D) 800 m
6. A body moving at 20 m/s slows uniformly to 5 m/s in 3 s. Acceleration: A) −5 m/s² B) −3 m/s² C) 5 m/s² D) 15 m/s²
7. For uniform acceleration, which equation is valid? A) v=u+at B) v=u−s C) s=ut² D) a=uv
8. A cyclist covers 2 km in 5 minutes. Average speed in m/s is: A) 4.67 B) 5.67 C) 6.67 D) 7.67
9. A velocity–time graph is a horizontal line above the time axis. The body has: A) zero velocity B) constant positive velocity C) increasing acceleration D) negative displacement always
10. A car travels equal distances at 30 km/h and 60 km/h. Average speed is: A) 45 km/h B) 40 km/h C) 50 km/h D) 35 km/h

## Chapter 2 — Force and Laws of Motion

1. SI unit of force is: A) joule B) newton C) watt D) pascal
2. A 4 kg object accelerates at 3 m/s². Net force is: A) 7 N B) 12 N C) 1.33 N D) 24 N
3. Inertia depends primarily on: A) speed B) mass C) shape D) colour
4. Action and reaction forces act on: A) same object B) different objects C) only stationary objects D) no objects
5. Momentum of a 2 kg body moving at 5 m/s is: A) 2.5 kg m/s B) 7 kg m/s C) 10 kg m/s D) 25 kg m/s
6. A 10 N net force acts on a 2 kg body for 3 s from rest. Final speed: A) 5 m/s B) 10 m/s C) 15 m/s D) 30 m/s
7. A 0.5 kg ball changes velocity by 8 m/s in 0.2 s. Average force magnitude: A) 2 N B) 10 N C) 20 N D) 40 N
8. Seat belts protect passengers mainly because they: A) increase inertia B) reduce the time over which momentum changes C) increase speed D) remove mass
9. If net external force is zero, total momentum of an isolated system is: A) zero always B) conserved C) doubled D) lost
10. A 2 kg cart at 4 m/s collides and sticks to a stationary 6 kg cart. Common velocity after collision: A) 0.5 m/s B) 0.8 m/s C) 1 m/s D) 2 m/s

## Chapter 3 — Gravitation

1. Gravitational force between two masses increases when: A) distance increases B) either mass increases C) both masses decrease D) masses become colourless
2. Weight of a body is: A) its mass B) gravitational force on it C) its volume D) density only
3. Approximate value of g near Earth's surface is: A) 0.98 m/s² B) 9.8 m/s² C) 98 m/s² D) 980 m/s²
4. If distance between two masses doubles, gravitational force becomes: A) twice B) half C) one-fourth D) four times
5. Mass of an object on the Moon compared with Earth is: A) one-sixth B) six times C) unchanged D) zero
6. Pressure is defined as: A) force × area B) force/area C) mass/volume D) work/time
7. Density of a body of mass 300 g and volume 100 cm³ is: A) 0.3 g/cm³ B) 3 g/cm³ C) 30 g/cm³ D) 300 g/cm³
8. An object floats in a liquid when its average density is generally: A) greater than liquid B) less than liquid C) infinite D) unrelated to liquid density
9. Buoyant force on an immersed object acts: A) downward B) upward C) horizontally only D) always zero
10. A stone weighs 20 N in air and 15 N when immersed. Upthrust is: A) 5 N B) 15 N C) 20 N D) 35 N

## Chapter 4 — Work and Energy

1. Work done by a constant force parallel to displacement is: A) Fs B) F/s C) s/F D) zero always
2. SI unit of work is: A) watt B) joule C) newton D) kilogram
3. A 10 N force moves an object 3 m in its direction. Work: A) 3 J B) 10 J C) 30 J D) 13 J
4. Kinetic energy is: A) mv B) mv² C) ½mv² D) mgh²
5. A 2 kg object moves at 3 m/s. Its kinetic energy is: A) 3 J B) 6 J C) 9 J D) 18 J
6. Gravitational potential energy near Earth's surface is: A) mgh B) ½mv² C) F/s D) Pt²
7. A 100 W device runs for 10 s. Energy transferred: A) 10 J B) 100 J C) 1000 J D) 10,000 J
8. Power is: A) work/time B) force/area C) mass × speed D) energy × time
9. If speed doubles, kinetic energy becomes: A) double B) triple C) four times D) half
10. In the absence of non-conservative losses, total mechanical energy: A) is conserved B) always increases C) always becomes zero D) equals momentum

## Answer key and short reasoning

**Motion:** 1 B (150/10); 2 C (round trip); 3 C (v=0+2×5); 4 B; 5 A; 6 A ((5−20)/3); 7 A; 8 C (2000/300); 9 B; 10 B (equal-distance average = 2uv/(u+v)=40).
**Force:** 1 B; 2 B (F=ma); 3 B; 4 B; 5 C; 6 C (a=5 m/s², v=15 m/s); 7 C (F=mΔv/Δt); 8 B; 9 B; 10 C (initial momentum 8 kg·m/s; total mass 8 kg, so v=1 m/s).
**Gravitation:** 1 B; 2 B; 3 B; 4 C; 5 C; 6 B; 7 B; 8 B; 9 B; 10 A.
**Work and Energy:** 1 A; 2 B; 3 C; 4 C; 5 C; 6 A; 7 C; 8 A; 9 C; 10 A.
