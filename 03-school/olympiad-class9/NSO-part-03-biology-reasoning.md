# NSO/ISO Practice Bank — Part 3: Biology and Reasoning

## 10. Cell — The Fundamental Unit of Life
1. The basic structural and functional unit of life is the: A tissue B cell C organ D organ system
2. The cell membrane is: A freely permeable to everything B selectively permeable C a rigid cellulose wall in every cell D absent in animal cells
3. The organelle associated with cellular respiration and ATP production is: A ribosome B mitochondrion C Golgi apparatus D vacuole
4. The control centre containing most hereditary material in a typical eukaryotic cell is: A nucleus B lysosome C cell wall D chloroplast
5. Plant cells typically have a cell wall made mainly of: A chitin B cellulose C glycogen D protein only
6. Ribosomes are the site of: A protein synthesis B photosynthesis C lipid storage only D water transport
7. Chloroplasts are usually found in: A mature mammalian red blood cells B photosynthetic plant cells C all bacteria D animal muscle cells
8. A prokaryotic cell lacks a: A plasma membrane B cytoplasm C membrane-bound nucleus D genetic material
9. Osmosis is movement of water through a selectively permeable membrane from: A lower water potential to higher water potential B higher water potential to lower water potential C solid to gas D nucleus to ribosome
10. Which organelle helps digest waste and worn-out cell parts? A lysosome B chloroplast C centrosome D nucleolus
