# IMO Practice Bank — Part 1
**Class 9 | Original practice questions | 10 questions per chapter**

Try each chapter in 10–15 minutes. Choose one answer per question. Answer key with short reasoning is at the end.

## Chapter 1 — Number Systems

1. Which is irrational? A) 0.125 B) \(\sqrt{81}\) C) \(\sqrt{18}\) D) \(7/11\)
2. Simplify \((\sqrt5+\sqrt2)(\sqrt5-\sqrt2)\). A) 7 B) 3 C) \(\sqrt3\) D) 10
3. Which lies between \(\sqrt{10}\) and \(\sqrt{11}\)? A) 3.1 B) 3.4 C) 3.5 D) 3.6
4. If \(x=\sqrt3+\sqrt2\), find \(x^2+1/x^2\). A) 8 B) 10 C) 12 D) 14
5. The decimal expansion of a rational number is always: A) terminating only B) non-terminating only C) terminating or recurring D) non-recurring
6. Rationalise \(1/(\sqrt7-\sqrt5)\). A) \((\sqrt7-\sqrt5)/2\) B) \((\sqrt7+\sqrt5)/2\) C) \(\sqrt{35}/2\) D) 2
7. Which statement is true? A) Sum of two irrationals is always irrational B) Product of two irrationals is always irrational C) A rational plus an irrational is irrational D) Every real number is rational
8. If \(a=0.\overline{27}\), then \(a\) equals: A) 27/100 B) 3/11 C) 27/90 D) 2/7
9. Evaluate \(\sqrt{50}-\sqrt8\). A) \(3\sqrt2\) B) \(7\sqrt2\) C) \(\sqrt{42}\) D) 2
10. If \(p=\sqrt2\), which is rational? A) p+1 B) p² C) 2p D) p/2

6. If \(p(x)=2x^3-x+5\), then \(p(-1)\) is: A) 2 B) 4 C) 6 D) 8
7. A polynomial of degree 1 has at most how many zeroes? A) 0 B) 1 C) 2 D) infinitely many

9. Which expression is not a polynomial in x? A) \(x^2+1\) B) \(3x^{-1}+2\) C) \(5x-7\) D) 9
