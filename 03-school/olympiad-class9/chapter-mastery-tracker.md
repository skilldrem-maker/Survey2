# Class 9 IMO + NSO/ISO Chapter Mastery Tracker
Use this with the first-round and challenge-round question banks. Fill in scores yourself after each timed attempt.

## How to use it
- **Round 1:** answer the first 10 MCQs for a section without notes.
- **Round 2:** answer the 5 extra challenge MCQs without notes.
- **Mastery target:** 10/10 on Round 1 and 5/5 on Round 2 after correcting mistakes. If you miss a question, write the concept—not just the option—and retry it the next day.
- Do not copy answers while solving. Use the key only after finishing the attempt.
