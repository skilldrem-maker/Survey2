## Quality checklist for every worksheet
- 100 numbered, original four-option MCQs
- A mix of concept, application, calculation, experiment/data interpretation, and higher-order questions where appropriate
- Answer key with a final count of 100 questions
- Check calculations and answer-key alignment before marking a worksheet complete
- Cross-check chapter coverage against the current official SOF syllabus and NCERT material

## Exam-pattern reminder (SOF 2026–27 Level 1)
The official pattern is 50 questions in 60 minutes. IMO: 15 Logical Reasoning, 20 Mathematical Reasoning, 10 Everyday Mathematics, 5 Achievers. NSO/ISO: 10 Logical Reasoning, 35 Science, 5 Achievers. Level 1 includes current- and previous-class syllabus material; Achievers questions use current-class material.
